SparkRush: Car Survival

Privacy policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026

SparkRush: Car Survival stores your game progress so you can continue playing. You do not need to create a separate game account. This version has no connected advertising, tracking or analytics service and no developer operated game server.

Progress on your device

The game saves your deposited coin balance, cars, upgrades, completed levels, prepared boosts, settings and purchase delivery records on your device. Temporary files and safety copies help recover from interrupted or damaged saves. A run that is still in progress and coins you have not deposited are not restored after the app closes.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. The game does not receive your payment card details. It receives verified transaction information, including the product, transaction identifier and status, so it can deliver coins, avoid duplicate delivery and handle refunds. Purchase records are retained with your save for these purposes. Resetting gameplay does not erase all purchase records.

Private iCloud saves

When iCloud saving is available and you are signed into iCloud, the game stores a copy of your saved progress and purchase records in your private CloudKit database. It uses this copy to sync and recover progress on devices using the same iCloud account. The game uses account and revision identifiers to keep saves and purchase delivery consistent. Your private save is not posted publicly or sent to a developer operated server.

Apple provides iCloud and StoreKit under its own privacy terms. See Apple Privacy.

Changes made while disconnected remain on the device until they sync. When two saves conflict, the game asks which complete save to keep. It does not add their coin balances together.

File backups

The game writes or reads a backup outside the app only when you choose a file location. Files you save to another app, cloud provider or recipient are also subject to that service's handling of the file. A file backup remains at the chosen location until you delete it.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and recovery copies remain in the app's storage until they are replaced or removed. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Private iCloud data and exported backups are separate and may remain after the app is deleted.

You can manage the game's iCloud data through your device's iCloud storage settings. To prevent another installation from uploading its local save again, stop using iCloud saving for the game on your other devices before deleting the cloud data. Delete any exported backup files separately. Apple may retain its own payment records according to its policies.

The game does not operate a separate user account to delete. If you need help managing or deleting your save, contact the support address shown below.

Support correspondence

If you choose to contact the developer, the developer receives the contact details and information you send. Send only information needed to understand your issue. Never send an Apple password, verification code or payment card details. Support messages are used to answer your request. Requests to delete support correspondence can be sent to the same address, subject to any records that must be retained.
