SparkRush: Car Survival

Support

Playing

Drag to steer. Your vehicle fires automatically when enemies are in range. In survival mode, every run starts at level 1. The level advances automatically every 30 seconds while you keep driving. Enemies and hazards build as you survive. Death ends the run; choose Restart to begin again at level 1.

In survival mode, coins you collect are added to your wallet during play and remain yours if you die or exit a run. Free map trials do not change your saved wallet. Survival progress is saved regularly and when you pause or end a run. An unexpected shutdown may lose the last few seconds of unsaved progress. The unfinished run itself does not resume after reopening the app.

Pause offers Resume, Restart and Exit. Restart and Exit ask for confirmation. Resume continues the same run. Restart begins a new run at level 1 and keeps coins already collected. Exit ends the run, keeps collected coins and returns to the main menu. Used boosts stay spent; unused prepared boosts remain ready. Shop and Garage are available from the main menu.

Cars and upgrades have level requirements. Buying coins does not skip these requirements. All coin packs are optional, and coins can also be earned by playing.

Missing coins after a purchase

If the coin shop shows Retry for an unfinished or unavailable purchase, select it to check the purchase again. Do not buy the same pack again just to recover an unfinished purchase. Check the purchase status with Apple and wait for any approval request to be completed. A cancelled purchase does not grant coins. A pending purchase grants coins after approval and verified delivery.

If iCloud reports a conflict or an account change, resolve that message in Settings, then Saves & iCloud. Coin purchases may remain unavailable while the save needs attention.

Desert Starter Pack

The optional Starter Pack permanently unlocks the Desert map and Copper Spark appearance. It contains no coins and does not change vehicle stats. Open Shop, select Starter Pack and use Restore Purchases with the same Apple account to restore this purchase. Consumable coin packs cannot be restored this way.

Game Center leaderboard

Open Leaderboard from the home screen, connect to Game Center and choose View Global. The separate survival leaderboard records the highest level reached in a run. There is no final campaign level, and older campaign scores are not copied to this leaderboard. Practice map trials do not change your survival record. Use Share Best Level to submit an existing local record if it is not yet linked to your Game Center account. New survival records are submitted automatically for the linked account, and pending scores retry after reconnection.

Game Center and iCloud are separate Apple services. Game Center shares your leaderboard score and profile name according to Apple's privacy settings; iCloud syncs your private saved progress. Resetting progress inside the game does not remove a score already on Game Center.

Saves and iCloud

Open Settings, then Saves & iCloud. Wait for a successful sync before moving to another device. Use the same iCloud account. Your saved wallet includes coins collected during play. The active run is not restored after relaunch, though an unexpected shutdown can lose the last few seconds that had not yet been saved.

If two saves differ, review the displayed progress carefully before choosing which complete save to keep. The game does not combine their coin balances. Export a backup before troubleshooting a save. Avoid deleting the app while investigating missing progress.

Sound

Check the iPhone's silent mode and device volume, then the game's Master, Music and Effects sliders. Check whether audio is being sent to headphones or a Bluetooth device.

Report a problem

Tell us the device model, iOS version, game version, the screen or level involved and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot or short recording can help. For purchases, include the product name and what the app or Apple displayed. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment card details.
