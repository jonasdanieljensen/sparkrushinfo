SparkRush: Car Survival

Support

Playing

Drag to steer. Your car fires automatically when enemies are in range. Follow the mission objective and deposit your carried coins at a Coin Drop to keep them. Coins still being carried are lost if the attempt ends without depositing them.

Pause offers Resume, Restart and Exit. Resume continues the same attempt. Restart begins the same level again. Exit ends the attempt and returns to the main menu. Restart and Exit ask for confirmation and explain what will be lost. Shop and Garage are available from the main menu. Banked coins, completed purchases and saved progress are kept.

Cars and upgrades have level requirements. Buying coins does not skip these requirements. All coin packs are optional, and coins can also be earned by playing.

Missing coins after a purchase

Open Shop and select Retry. Do not buy the same pack again just to recover an unfinished purchase. Check the purchase status with Apple and wait for any approval request to be completed. A cancelled purchase does not grant coins. A pending purchase grants coins after approval and verified delivery.

If iCloud reports a conflict or an account change, resolve that message in Settings, then Saves & iCloud. Coin purchases may remain unavailable while the save needs attention.

Saves and iCloud

Open Settings, then Saves & iCloud. Wait for a successful sync before moving to another device. Use the same iCloud account. Unfinished runs and carried coins are not part of the saved progress.

If two saves differ, review the displayed progress carefully before choosing which complete save to keep. The game does not combine their coin balances. Export a backup before troubleshooting a save. Avoid deleting the app while investigating missing progress.

Sound

Check the iPhone's silent mode and device volume, then the game's Master, Music and Effects sliders. Check whether audio is being sent to headphones or a Bluetooth device.

Report a problem

Tell us the device model, iOS version, game version, the screen or level involved and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot or short recording can help. For purchases, include the product name and what the app or Apple displayed. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment card details.
